# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing real exploit material, secrets, or unauthorized-access guidance via project channels.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Ethical-use expectation
Because this is a security project, the community also expects members to uphold authorized-use
principles (see `DISCLAIMER.md`). Using project channels to solicit, plan, or boast about
unauthorized testing is not tolerated.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers via the
contact in the repository profile or a private security advisory. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban
contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.
