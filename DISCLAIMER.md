# Disclaimer — Authorized Use Only

`claude-vapt-lab` is a toolkit for **authorized** security testing and education. By using it you
agree to the following.

## Permitted use
- Testing systems **you own**.
- Testing systems for which you hold **explicit, written authorization** from the owner (see
  `scope/authorization-letter-template.md`).
- Intentionally vulnerable **practice ranges** (DVWA, OWASP Juice Shop, WebGoat, VulnHub) and
  legitimate training platforms (Hack The Box, TryHackMe) within their own rules of engagement.

## Prohibited use
- Accessing, scanning, probing, or exploiting any system without authorization.
- Testing third-party, customer, partner, or production systems you do not own and are not
  authorized to test.
- Any use that violates applicable laws or regulations (e.g. computer-misuse / unauthorized-access
  statutes in your jurisdiction).

**not liable** for any damage, data loss, service disruption, legal consequence, or other harm
arising from its use or misuse. **You are solely responsible** for ensuring your testing is lawful
and authorized.

## Not legal advice
The authorization-letter template and rules-of-engagement are starting points, not legal advice.
Adapt them to your jurisdiction and have them reviewed where appropriate.

## Responsible disclosure
If you discover a vulnerability in a system you are authorized to test, follow responsible /
coordinated disclosure with the asset owner. Do not publish exploit details that put users at risk
before a fix is available.

Unauthorized access to computer systems is a crime in most countries. **When in doubt, get written
permission first.**
